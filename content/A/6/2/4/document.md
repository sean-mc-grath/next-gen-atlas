##### A.6.2.4 - Collateralization And Tokenization Constraints [Core]

Halo Agents are intended to be the primary venue for leverage on advanced or exotic assets in the Sky Ecosystem. Halo tokens can be made eligible as collateral in the [Allocation System](9db14ab7-bb4b-4751-8084-843bd4359f2a), subject to the collateral eligibility framework and risk parameters specified in the Allocation System and collateral parameter Articles referenced by Prime and Generator Agent Artifacts. A Halo token that does not meet these requirements may continue to operate as a product, but cannot be used as collateral for Allocation System positions until it is brought back within the permitted ranges.

Detailed numerical parameters governing Halo token collateralization—including minimum free float, maximum permitted ownership concentration by any single Prime Agent, haircuts, and capacity limits—are defined in the relevant Allocation System and collateral parameter provisions and may be updated through the standard risk governance processes. Changes to those parameters are not controlled unilaterally by any single Halo Agent.

Halo Agents may exist in tokenized or non-tokenized forms. Non-tokenized Halo Agents that remain directly controlled by an administrator and do not issue governance tokens may be treated differently for fee and collateral purposes than tokenized Halos, as specified in the [Agent Creation Fees](1b1c9cc0-e410-4bb3-aa37-c639ca392dd7) and collateral framework Articles.
