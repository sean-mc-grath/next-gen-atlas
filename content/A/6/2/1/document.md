##### A.6.2.1 - Definition And Purpose [Core]

Halo Agents are Sky Agents whose Agent Artifacts are enveloped inside the Agent Artifacts of a Prime Agent (see [A.0.1.1.47 - Halo Agent (HA)](e6261608-c225-452a-aca7-6eb7b5ed2d58) and [A.0.1.1.45 - Prime Agent](a8454271-c090-4084-b022-4430e3def93c)). They provide governed tokenization and product wrappers for external assets, business models, or other idiosyncratic value, so that these can be held, monitored, and allocated to through the Sky Ecosystem.

Halo Agents operate within the standardized smart contract, risk, data, and legal infrastructure defined by the [Laniakea](840ea6eb-2b68-4bec-86fc-9f9ca965421f) framework and are monitored through [Sentinel](1b6393b1-7f7f-48ac-b7a0-13f148be290d), so that Prime Agents and the wider governance system can evaluate Halo-based products on the same risk basis as other Sky capital products.
