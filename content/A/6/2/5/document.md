##### A.6.2.5 - Lifecycle And Governance Interfaces [Core]

Halo Agents typically begin as internal products or artifacts within a Prime Agent, with limited scope and no independent tokenization. As a product matures and demonstrates a stable mandate and operational track record, it may be spun out as a distinct Halo Agent with its own Agent Artifacts and, optionally, a token that allows external participants to hold its economics and risk.

The creation, modification, and deprecation of Halo Agents require governance actions by the Prime Agents that envelop them, together with any Generator Agents, Executor Agents, or [Core Council](5a03a0c4-a47a-409c-9b23-52ac93e63d45) roles specified in their Artifacts and Accords. The Agent Creation Fee mechanism applies to tokenized Halo Agents as defined in the [Agent Creation Fees](1b1c9cc0-e410-4bb3-aa37-c639ca392dd7) provisions, including any exemptions for Halos incubated by Genesis Agents under specified ownership conditions. Prime Agents must ensure that the nested Halo Agent Artifacts described in [A.6.1.1.1 - Nested Halo Agent Artifacts](7f957852-7618-4c2f-9e11-76ee01a2ab25) and its sub-documents are kept up to date, so that [Sentinel](1b6393b1-7f7f-48ac-b7a0-13f148be290d) and other governance processes can rely on a consistent, accessible representation of each Halo's mandate, limits, and reporting obligations.
