###### A.6.1.1.1.4 - Reporting And Sentinel Integration [Core]

Prime Agent Artifacts must include, for each enveloped [Halo Agent](e6261608-c225-452a-aca7-6eb7b5ed2d58), references to the Halo's reporting and [Sentinel](1b6393b1-7f7f-48ac-b7a0-13f148be290d) integration Artifacts, so that the Prime's Sentinel configuration can locate and verify the data published by the Halo.
