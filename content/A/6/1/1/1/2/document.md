###### A.6.1.1.1.2 - Halo Token Issuance And Prime Limits [Core]

Prime Agent Artifacts must include, for each enveloped [A.0.1.1.47 - Halo Agent](e6261608-c225-452a-aca7-6eb7b5ed2d58), the list of Halo tokens issued (if any) and any Prime-specific limits or constraints on holding or using those tokens.
