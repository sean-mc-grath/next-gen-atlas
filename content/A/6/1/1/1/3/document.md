###### A.6.1.1.1.3 - Risk Limits And Collateral Eligibility [Core]

Prime Agent Artifacts must include, for each enveloped [A.0.1.1.47 - Halo Agent](e6261608-c225-452a-aca7-6eb7b5ed2d58), references to the Halo's risk and limits Artifacts and to the collateral eligibility parameters that apply to the Halo token, as set in the [A.2.2.9.1 - Allocation System](9db14ab7-bb4b-4751-8084-843bd4359f2a) and collateral parameter Articles.
