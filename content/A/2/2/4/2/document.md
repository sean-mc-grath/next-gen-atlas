###### A.2.2.4.2 - Prime Transformation Primitive [Core]

Prior to Activating this Primitive, a [A.0.1.1.48 - Proto-Agent](10362c8d-5789-4018-baf6-dc1834e1297d) has been created, meaning it has not yet adopted any specialized role. Since a Proto-Agent cannot perform actions in the Sky Ecosystem, it must first transform into a specialized Agent sub-type, such as a Prime Agent or Executor Agent, to gain functionality. The Prime Transformation Primitive defined herein allows an Agent to transform into a Prime Agent, subject to certain conditions.
