###### A.2.2.4.5 - Generator Transformation Primitive [Core]

The Generator Transformation Primitive will allow an Agent to transform into a [A.0.1.1.44 - Generator Agent](7535ee4b-1220-4459-9bdd-2830a611553c), subject to certain conditions. The formal definition of this Primitive will be specified in a future iteration of the Atlas.
