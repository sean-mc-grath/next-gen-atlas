###### A.0.1.1.59 - Sky Generated Asset [Core]

A Sky Generated Asset is an asset created by the protocol through the [Generate Sky Asset Primitive](6ae9b429-5872-4c18-831e-77f2d5ad7b64) under parameters set by Sky Governance. Sky Generated Assets include stablecoins, pegged assets, and synthetic assets such as USDS, each with its own risk framework, distribution strategy, and bridge-security design defined in the Agent Artifacts of the [Generator Agent](7535ee4b-1220-4459-9bdd-2830a611553c) responsible for that asset.
