###### A.0.1.1.61 - Generate Sky Asset Primitive [Core]

The Generate Sky Asset Primitive is a [Sky Primitive](ae14941a-635e-4022-af4d-2bec2827fbbf) that allows authorized [Generator Agents](7535ee4b-1220-4459-9bdd-2830a611553c) to mint and burn units of a specific [Sky Generated Asset](c7b49829-2b73-440b-b958-6bee667d596f) in accordance with governance-defined rules. It enforces supply, risk, and accounting constraints for each Sky Generated Asset and connects the asset's lifecycle to the broader Sky risk and capital framework.
