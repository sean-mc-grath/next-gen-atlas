###### A.0.1.1.62 - Sentinel [Core]

Sentinel is the unified control, trading, and compliance backbone of the Sky Ecosystem. It is an open-source, modular agentic process stack that continuously gathers data, verifies compliance, and executes actions on behalf of [A.0.1.1.45 - Prime Agents](a8454271-c090-4084-b022-4430e3def93c), [A.0.1.1.47 - Halo Agents](e6261608-c225-452a-aca7-6eb7b5ed2d58), [A.0.1.1.46 - Executor Agents](ac514975-66ad-4b43-8f76-42cac5ca599d), and the [A.0.1.1.51 - Core Council](5a03a0c4-a47a-409c-9b23-52ac93e63d45), using standardized components such as verification, reporting, settlement, and execution modules that read from and write to [A.0.1.1.40 - Agent Artifacts](8d081c1a-6393-4aaf-8914-8959cdf2fee3) and Synome.
