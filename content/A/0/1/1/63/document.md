###### A.0.1.1.63 - Laniakea [Core]

Laniakea is the infrastructure framework through which Sky's capital is allocated and managed by Primes and Halos. It standardizes the smart contracts, risk accounting, data infrastructure, and legal scaffolding used by Sky Agents, so that new capital products and tokenization systems can be launched as governed, incremental extensions of a shared architecture rather than bespoke constructions.
